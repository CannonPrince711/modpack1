# Dungeon Delver's Odyssey

Forge 1.20.1 RPG pack configuration: three KubeJS classes (plus the RPG Series), boss-kill
progression with coin bounties, Apotheosis dungeon-tier affixes, a scripted royal opening in a
walled town and castle, quest chapters, a balance worksheet, and a Modrinth pack builder.

**Mods:** 142 of a 150 cap (`mods.lock.json`). Forge 47.4.0, Java 17. `optionalmods.md` lists
mods that sit just outside the pack.

## Build and install

1. Use Java 17 and a Modrinth-compatible launcher.
2. Run `python3 tools/build_pack.py` to create `Dungeon-Delvers-Odyssey.mrpack` (it runs the tests first).
3. Import the `.mrpack` and allocate at least 8 GB RAM.
4. FTB Quests, FTB Library, FTB Teams, Item Filters, MineColonies, Structurize, BlockUI and Domum
   Ornamentum install from pinned CurseForge files; everything else from Modrinth. All are
   listed with checksums and source metadata in `mods.lock.json`.

The `.mrpack` includes all pinned Modrinth and CurseForge game mods and required dependencies.
Forge 47.4.0 is the loader pin. Each lock entry may carry an `env` (`client` and `server`:
required, optional or unsupported); the builder writes it into the index, so client-only mods
(Embeddium, Oculus, minimaps, FPS and sound mods) are not installed on a dedicated server. Client
and server must use the same files. Create a new world after installation: structure and cave
generation changes do not retrofit chunks.

## The opening

On a fresh world the first server tick builds, around world spawn: a moated castle with a
drawbridge, gatehouse, round towers, wall walks, a keep and throne hall, plus a timber-framed town
with a market, tavern, chapel with bell tower, smithy, farms and an arrival waystone. The player
is held behind a black loading screen until it stands, then:

1. a title card and a slow aerial orbit of the castle;
2. the player arrives at the waystone; guards shout (in three different voices), sprint in and seize them;
3. they are hauled through the town, gate and courtyard into the throne hall;
4. King Aldric briefs them and sends them to clear the crypt beneath the market well;
5. killing the warden (a deliberately easy husk) returns them to the king, who sets them free
   with a purse and diamonds.

Voices were generated with ElevenLabs on 2026-10-07 from library voices (King Aldric: Elder
Rowan; guards: Jerry B., Jase, Garrison) and live in `overrides/kubejs/assets/ddo/`. Check
ElevenLabs' terms for the account and voices before redistributing the pack.

Everything here is generated, not hand-built: `tools/gen_castle.py` writes the tile structures and
the layout the cutscene follows, and refuses to finish unless a pathfinder can walk from the far end
of the town to the throne (the moat can be crossed only by the bridge). `tools/gen_trapvault.py`
builds the crypt, which is also a natural structure (`ddo:trapvault`) with plate, potion and
trapped-chest traps.

## Play

Choose a starter class in chat with `!class warrior`, `!class mage`, or `!class ranger`. Other
chat commands: `!shop` (where the Quartermaster stands), `!dungeon` (back to the crypt during the
first expedition), `!skipintro`. The class script applies permanent class and level attribute
modifiers; Mage also gets +15% on Iron's Spells and Spell Engine spell power. Curios slots are
restricted by class: Warrior (ring, belt, hands), Mage (ring, necklace, charm, spellbook), and
Ranger (ring, belt, back, charm). Sophisticated Backpacks are exempt, and the Spell Engine slots
(`spell_book`, `spell_scroll`, `spell_trinket`, `spell_quiver`) are open to every class so the RPG
Series works. Items found in a disallowed equipped slot are returned to the player's inventory
every second.

Boss sequence for the campaign and regression model:

1. Ancient Remnant — Overworld ruins / introductory raid.
2. Netherite Monstrosity — Nether catacombs.
3. Ender Guardian — End citadel.

The boss rewards are cosmetic, named vanilla items. Every player within 64 blocks in the same
dimension receives that boss's progression XP and cosmetic, so all four players in a party advance
together. FTB Teams can share quest progress once installed.

### Coins and the shop

Bosses also pay **coins** (Lightman's Currency): Overworld bosses gold coins, Nether bosses emerald,
End bosses more emerald (`progression.js`). The Odyssey Quartermaster, a stationary villager at
world spawn, sells the same tiered modded gear that appears in loot, one coin type per price
(`shop.js`, generated by `tools/gen_shop.py`).

## Gear balance and loot

The pack's tiers are iron-class (Overworld), diamond-class (Nether) and netherite-class (End).
`tools/gen_gear_rebalance.py` reads a server dump of item attributes and the mod jars and audits
every modded weapon and armor piece (power is damage per second for weapons, armor plus toughness
per slot for armor) into `balance/gear-tier-audit.csv`. A craftable item may not outclass its
materials: one tier over has its main material lifted one step (iron to diamond, diamond to
netherite); two or more is loot-only. Smithing upgrades and recipes that need unique boss
materials are left alone. The result is `recipes.js`.

`tools/gen_gear_loot.py` validates a curated list against that worksheet, then writes the
Apotheosis affix entries, three `ddo:gear/<tier>` loot tables and `gear_loot.js`, which adds them
to about 430 dungeon chests (KubeJS `genericLootTables`; the chest event silently prefixes
`chests/` to ids). `tools/fix_loot_tables.py` repairs tables that Forge discards because they
reference absent mods, and `tools/gen_structure_fit.py` keeps overlapping structure systems apart
(strongholds, jungle and swamp towns).

The loot worksheet at `balance/difficulty-loot-balance.csv` is the balancing source of truth.
Apotheosis item-affix drops target the pinned YUNG, When Dungeons Arise, Cataclysm, Dungeons and
Taverns, Integrated Dungeons and Structures and vanilla chest table IDs; rarity clamps step by
dimension. Rebalanced Enchanting is included to tune early villager trades, anvil progression, and
Mending. Its exact config/default behavior still needs in-game verification against the 1.20.1
release before public launch. Apotheosis rarity weights are 400/180/55/14/3/1 from common through
ancient; eligible affix ranges step upward from common-to-rare in the Overworld, rare-to-epic in
the Nether, and epic-to-mythic in the End. CSV top-rarity probabilities are target rates, not
measured chest-drop telemetry.

## What is and is not validated

`python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v` runs the deterministic balance regression across all
three classes and the first three campaign bosses, plus consistency tests for the lock, build
manifest, gear audit, loot, shop, castle and voice assets. It is not an in-game compatibility test.

In game, with the full mod set, on 2026-10-07: a Forge 47.4.0 dedicated server loads all
server-side mods and builds the castle from scratch; the Forge client plays the whole opening with
voices and then the crypt. Verified live: the recipe swaps and removals, the loot injection, the
trap vault's three traps, the Quartermaster and his 60 trades, and spot-checks of the moat, bridge,
waystones and NPCs. Not verified: combat balance against the new gear, the Epic Fight compat mod
against the pinned Epic Fight, Create: Structures (a 2023 beta) at depth, MineColonies play, and
the Nether and End expansions beyond loading. Known harmless log noise: Quark/Zeta creative-tab
warnings, placeholder L2 Weaponry data from the Epic Fight compat mod, and a few Samurai Dynasty
mask recipes.

**Not done:** MineColonies is installed but there are no pre-built colonies. A colony exists only
when a player places a Town Hall (it needs an owner and building data), so it cannot be generated
with a datapack or script.

## Pinned versions

`mods.lock.json` records each included Modrinth project/version ID, direct download URL, SHA-1,
SHA-512, size, and its required dependencies. These are immutable release pins; CurseForge entries
carry project/file IDs. Rebuilds do not silently advance mods. The one deliberate exception is
recorded in its entry (`release_filter_exception`): Create: Structures 0.1.1, the only 1.20.1 Forge
build, a 2023 beta. Battle Arts was removed because it crashes against the pinned Epic Fight
(details in `optionalmods.md`).
